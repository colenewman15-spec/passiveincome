# Reinvestment Policy

Goal: maximize compounding while keeping the system self-funding.

## Allocation (selected)

- **100% of net revenue reinvested until $500/month** average.
- After $500/month: 70% reinvest / 30% reserve.
- After $2,000/month: 50% reinvest / 30% reserve / 20% owner draw.
- Reserve funds are used only for: domain/hosting, content, backlinks, ad tests, or acquiring a small cash-flowing asset.

## Reinvestment order (highest ROI first)

1. **Domain + hosting** (~$15-30/yr) - makes the site portable and credible.
2. **Content that targets buyer-intent keywords** - highest compounding return.
3. **Backlinks / digital PR** - only after 20+ quality pages exist.
4. **Paid ads** - only to a page/offer already converting organically.
5. **Acquire a small site/newsletter** - at 3-4x annual profit, once reserve > $2,000.

## Rules

- Never spend on ads before organic conversion is proven.
- Never spend more than 20% of reserve on a single test.
- Track every dollar in `finance/ledger.csv` (auto-generated by `revenue_tracker.mjs`).
- Keep a 3-month hosting reserve at all times once revenue starts.

## Separate account

Revenue should land in a dedicated account (business checking or a separate savings sub-account), not your primary personal account. This keeps reinvestment money separate and makes bookkeeping simple. Opening it requires your identity/KYC; see `setup_separate_account.md`.
