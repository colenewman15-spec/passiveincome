## Strategy

1. Apply to 2 networks + 3 direct programs first (approval takes days to weeks).
2. Put affiliate links only where a product is relevant; disclose clearly.
3. Track clicks and conversions per page; double down on the pages that convert.
4. Do not add links to products you have not verified are available.

## Compliance

- FTC disclosure must be visible before the link (the pages already include a disclosure block).
- Follow each program's terms (no bidding on brand terms, no misleading claims).
- Do not invent reviews, ratings, or specifications.
