FAQ

What factors should I consider when choosing a CRM?

Key considerations include the size of your sales team, necessary integrations with existing tools like email or accounting software, budget constraints, and whether you need cloud-based or on-premise deployment.

Can I switch CRMs later if my needs change?

Yes, most modern CRM platforms allow for data export and migration. However, switching involves mapping data fields between systems and retraining staff, so it is important to choose a solution that can scale with your growth.

Do I need technical skills to implement a CRM?

Basic setup often requires minimal technical knowledge, but advanced configuration, custom report building, or integrating third-party apps may require IT resources or certified implementation partners.
