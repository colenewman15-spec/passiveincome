FAQ

How do I choose the right email marketing platform?

Evaluate your list size, required integrations, and specific automation needs. Compare feature sets against your budget and technical resources.

What is the difference between transactional and promotional emails?

Promotional emails are marketing campaigns sent to subscribers, while transactional emails are triggered by user actions like order confirmations or password resets.

Can I migrate my existing email list to a new platform?

Most platforms support CSV imports for subscriber lists. Ensure you have consent records and clean your list before transferring to avoid deliverability issues.
