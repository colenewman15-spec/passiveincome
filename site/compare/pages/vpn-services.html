How we evaluate

  • Encryption Standards: Assess the encryption protocols used, such as AES-256, to ensure data remains secure from interception.
  • Server Network Size: Evaluate the number of physical locations and total server count available for routing connections.
  • Logging Policy: Review the provider's commitment to a no-logs policy regarding user activity and connection data.
  • Protocol Support: Check compatibility with various operating systems and network environments, including support for WireGuard or OpenVPN.
  • Customer Support Channels: Identify available assistance methods such as live chat, email, or knowledge bases for technical issues.

FAQ

How does a VPN protect my data?

A VPN creates an encrypted tunnel between your device and the internet, preventing third parties from reading your transmitted data.

Can I use a VPN on mobile devices?

Most reputable providers offer dedicated applications for iOS and Android operating systems.

Do VPNs affect internet speed?

Encryption processes may introduce latency, and connection speeds depend on the distance to the nearest server node.
